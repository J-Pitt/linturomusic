# Street-artist apparel

Noted 2026-11-01.

Product idea for t-shirts and hoodies, modeled on a friend’s shoe company. He sourced every design printed on the shoes from local street artists in South America.

Same model here: all designs come from local street artists in Brooklyn and New York City. A lot of friends there are already making this kind of work. Market the clothes to other countries, positioned as a designer line.

Each artist gets their own page:

- Bio
- What inspires them
- The kinds of artwork they make
- Examples of that work printed on a hoodie, t-shirt, or similar
